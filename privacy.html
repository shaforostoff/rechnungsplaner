Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This application (“Rechnungsplaner”) is an offline app for planning gigs and issuing invoices for them, including the German e-invoicing formats (ZUGFeRD / Factur-X and XRechnung). We take user privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about how Rechnungsplaner handles data.

2. Data Collection and Use

Rechnungsplaner does not collect personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, or advertising identifiers. It does not request the INTERNET permission, has no network access, and cannot transmit your data to us or to any server. Nothing you enter is ever seen by the developer.

Everything the app works with is entered by you and kept in the app's private on-device storage (inaccessible to other apps):

  • Your own details — business name, address, contact details, VAT ID (USt-IdNr) and tax number, bank details (IBAN, BIC, account holder), and your tax and payment-term defaults, as required to appear on an invoice.
  • Your customers — venue and registered name, address, contact person, email, phone, VAT ID, customer number, buyer reference and your own notes.
  • Gigs, services and invoices — dates, places, fees, line items, and the generated invoice files, which are archived in app-private storage so a past invoice can be re-sent years later exactly as it was issued.
  • Your preferences — output format, file-name and invoice-number patterns, interface and invoice language, the chosen calendar and the chosen export folder.

This data is stored purely so the app can do its job. It is not profiled, analysed, or used for any purpose other than producing your invoices and showing your own records back to you.

3. Permissions

Rechnungsplaner requests only two permissions, and they serve a single optional feature:

  • Calendar access (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR) — used only if you turn on calendar mirroring and pick a calendar. The app then writes your gigs into that calendar as ordinary events (title, location, your notes, and a short marker line that lets the app recognise its own events again), updates or removes those events when a gig changes, and reads events back from that one calendar over the date range you ask it to import, so you can adopt entries you made elsewhere. It does not read any other calendar, and it never reads your calendar unless you asked for an import or for mirroring.

The app requests no storage permission. Saving invoices and backups uses the Android system folder picker (Storage Access Framework): the app can only write to the folder you chose yourself. Sharing an invoice hands the single file you selected to the app you pick from the share sheet, through a read-only link limited to one directory.

4. When Data Leaves Your Device

The app itself has no way to send anything anywhere. Data leaves your device only through actions you take, and only to destinations you choose:

  • Calendar mirroring — if the calendar you pick belongs to an online account (for example a Google account), Android's own calendar sync will upload those events to that account, just as it does for events you create in your calendar app. Those events contain the gig's title, place and your notes. This is handled by Android and by that account provider under their privacy policies, not by Rechnungsplaner. Choosing a local, on-device calendar avoids it entirely.
  • Sharing an invoice — the generated PDF and/or XML is passed to the app you choose (mail app, messenger, and so on), with the customer's address and your message text pre-filled for mail apps. What happens next is governed by that app and by your mail provider.
  • Saving and exporting — invoices, the contacts export and the full backup archive are written to the folder you picked. If that folder belongs to a cloud storage provider, your files go there. These files contain personal data of your customers and, in the case of a backup, your own bank details.
  • Device backup — Android may include the app's data in your device's own backup if you have backups switched on. That is a platform feature governed by your device settings and your account provider; the app takes no active part in it.

5. Third-Party Sharing

Rechnungsplaner does not share any data with third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or external services. It contains no ads, no trackers and no third-party SDKs. The contacts export produces a file in the shape the lexoffice API accepts, but the app does not contact lexoffice or any other service — the file is simply saved for you to use as you see fit.

6. Security

Your database, the invoice archive and your settings live in app-private storage, which other apps on the device cannot read. Files offered to the share sheet are copied into a single directory that is served read-only, so a share cannot be turned into access to your invoice history. Because the app has no network access, there is no transmission of your data that could be intercepted.

Files you export or share leave this protection by design. Keeping exported invoices, contact exports and backup archives safe is up to you and the storage or messaging service you send them to.

7. Your Customers' Data

The customer details you enter are yours, and under the GDPR you are the controller of them. The developer has no access to them, receives no copy of them, and cannot retrieve or delete them for you. You can edit or delete any customer, gig or invoice in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes its data from the device — bearing in mind that invoices you have already issued are normally subject to statutory retention periods.
